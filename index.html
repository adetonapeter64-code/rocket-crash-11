🚀 About Rocket Crash

Rocket Crash is a crash game. The Rocket Crash takes flight in the beginning of the game. Cashout before it flies away to win! It is very easy to play with the following 3 steps.

1. Place a BET before the round starts.

2. WATCH the coefficient increase as the rocket continues to fly.

3. CASHOUT before the rocket flies away to win X times your money.

(X = the coefficient where you cashed out)

COEFFICIENTS

  1. The win coefficients starts at 1x and grows more and more as the rocket flies.
  2. Your winnings are calculated as: “Your Bet x The coefficient you cashed out at”.

BET & CASHOUT

  1. Select the amount and press the BET button to make a Bet.
  2. Press the CASH OUT button to cash out your winnings.
  3. Your BET is lost if you didn’t cash out before the rocket flies away.
